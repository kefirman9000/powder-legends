FAQ · How to play
  • Click the game to capture the mouse (touch screens need no capture). Only mouse movement counts, and your pose holds where you leave it.
  • Flick up = jump / stand · move down = crouch · right / left = tuck forward / lean back.
  • Backflip: lean back (move left) and crouch on the ramp, then stand up hard to pop off the lip. In the air, tuck to spin faster, stretch to slow down, and stand before landing.
  • A very hard fall onto your body knocks your skis clean off.
  • Hold the left mouse button to brake: it grips harder the longer you hold and throws up snow. Only works while your skis are on the ground. Right-click restarts.
  • On a phone or tablet: drag with ONE finger to move, and hold a SECOND finger down to brake. The finger you are moving with never brakes. Tap after a wipeout to restart.
  • Cross the finish line with your skis still on your feet and the level counts as finished, no matter how battered you are.
  • Hard landings and hits damage your skis. They darken as they take damage, and too much damage makes them snap off.
  • Even after a wipeout you can still wiggle a little with the mouse.
  • Finish a level for a rating from F up to S, plus awards for flips, combos, air time, landings and speed. Tucking tight (crouching) in the air makes you spin faster.
  • Scroll wheel or + / − / 0 = zoom · R retry · F fullscreen · M / Esc menu · 1–9 pick a level · N next level
  • On the level screen, use the arrow buttons or the ← / → keys to browse levels and Enter to play. Your best score and best grade for each level are saved.
