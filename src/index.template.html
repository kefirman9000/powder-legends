FAQ · How to play

Controls

  • New here? Play Ski School (level 1): six quick steps teach everything below.
  • Mouse: only movement counts and your pose holds where you leave it. Move down = crouch, flick up = jump / stand, left = lean back, right = tuck forward. Hold the left button to brake; right-click restarts.
  • Phone / tablet: drag with one finger to move; hold a second finger down to brake. Tap after a wipeout to restart.
  • Keys: R retry · M / Esc menu · N next level · 1–9 pick a level · F fullscreen · scroll or + / − / 0 zoom. In the menu: ← / → browse, Enter play.

Tricks & landings

  • Backflip: crouch on the ramp, stand up hard at the lip, then lean back and tuck in the air. Tuck tight to spin faster, stretch out to slow down.
  • Land with your skis flat on the slope: PERFECT pays ×1.25, GOOD ×1, SKETCHY half, and NOT FLAT pays nothing. Points only count once you've stayed up for a moment.
  • Big landings fill the adrenaline bar; keep it full for a rainbow trail. Hazards (fire walls, the shark pool, lava) pay a bonus when you clear them, more for a close shave.
  • Hard hits damage your skis (they darken) and too much snaps them off. Cross the finish with skis on and the level counts.
  • Reset Skis puts you back at the last checkpoint for 25,000 points, but A and LEGEND grades are off for that run.

Grades, records & extras

  • Every finish gets a grade from F up to LEGEND, plus awards for flips, air, landings, speed and style. Your best score and grade show on each level's card.
  • Turn on the ghost in Settings to race a see-through replay of your best run on each level.
  • Ski Jump Hill measures your distance and keeps a hill record. The Endless Run never ends: your score at the first crash is your record.
  • Finishing certain levels unlocks special gear (glowing skis, suits, helmets, trails). Wear it from Customize.
  • Replay any run from the results screen. Tap anywhere on the results to skip the tally.
  • Settings: graphics quality, ghost, vibration, updates and resetting scores. Updates show up as a banner on the menu.
